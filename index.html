Who I Am

I’m a results-driven Full Stack Developer and Computer Engineering professional with 4+ years of experience designing, developing, and deploying scalable web applications using JavaScript, PHP, Python, React, HTML, and CSS. I specialize in building accessible, user-centered digital solutions with a strong focus on clean architecture, performance, and maintainability.

I’m currently pursuing a master’s degree in computer engineering and networks.
